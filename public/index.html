OUR PURPOSE

MediCycle is a student-led field project created to spread awareness about unused medicines, medicine wastage and safe disposal practices.

Our vision A community that understands the value of medicines and the impact of improper disposal.
Our mission Promote awareness and connect people with verified participating services.

MediCycle is a student field-project platform, not a pharmacy or medicine redistribution service. Submitting a request does not mean a medicine is accepted or safe to reuse. Never give prescription medicines directly to another person. Acceptance and disposal decisions must be made by an appropriately authorized organization under applicable rules.
