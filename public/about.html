ABOUT MEDICYCLE

Every Medicine Matters.
Every Action Counts.

MediCycle is a college field project that promotes responsible medicine handling and awareness about unused medicine disposal. Our aim is to encourage informed decisions, reduce medicine wastage and protect the environment.

Who We Are

MediCycle is a student-led initiative focused on medicine-waste awareness. Many medicines remain unused after treatment. Improper disposal can create environmental risks. Through this platform, we aim to educate people about responsible handling and appropriate disposal guidance.

Our Mission

We aim to help people learn about safe disposal options and locate participating collection services. We want to spread awareness about medicine wastage and encourage people to seek guidance from appropriately authorized organizations.

Our Objectives

♡

Medicine Waste Awareness

Promote awareness about unused medicines and the problems caused by improper disposal.

♻

Responsible Disposal

Encourage people to follow appropriate medicine disposal guidance.

▤

Online Request Facility

Provide an online form to submit medicine details for review and guidance.

⌖

Collection Information

Help people find verified information about participating collection services.

🌱

Environmental Responsibility

Spread awareness about protecting the environment through responsible medicine handling.

✚

Community Awareness

Encourage communities to make informed decisions about medicines they no longer need.

Our Commitment to Medicine Safety

MediCycle is an awareness and request-review platform. It is not a pharmacy or an automatic medicine redistribution service.

Important Safety Notice

Medicine reuse is not automatically approved by MediCycle. Never share prescription medicines directly with another person. Collection, acceptance and disposal must be handled by an appropriately authorized organization under applicable regulations.
