Medicine Safety
Starts With You.

Medicines help us recover, but improper storage and disposal can create risks for people, animals and the environment. Learn how to handle unused medicines responsibly.

IMPORTANT GUIDELINES

Follow These Safety Practices

📅

1. Check Expiry Dates

Always check the expiry date before using any medicine. Never use expired medicines or offer them to someone else.

🔒

2. Store Medicines Safely

Keep medicines in their original packaging and follow the storage instructions. Keep them away from children and pets.

💊

3. Do Not Share Prescription Medicines

Prescription medicines should only be used by the person for whom they were prescribed. Never give them directly to another person.

♻️

4. Dispose of Medicines Responsibly

Do not flush medicines or throw them casually into household waste. Ask an authorized pharmacy or appropriate disposal service for instructions.

📦

5. Check Packaging Condition

Medicines with damaged, leaking or missing packaging should not be considered suitable for reuse. Ask an authorized service for guidance.

🌡️

6. Follow Storage Temperature

Some medicines need refrigeration or specific temperature conditions. Never assume they are safe after improper storage.

⚠ Important Safety Notice

Do not donate or redistribute prescription medicines directly to another person. Expired, opened, damaged or improperly stored medicines require appropriate disposal guidance. Collection and acceptance decisions must be made by an authorized organization under applicable rules.
